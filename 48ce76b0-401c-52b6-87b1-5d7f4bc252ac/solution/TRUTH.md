## Measured experiments behind this reference

### apportionment-residual-defect

Question: Why did the ordering policy that scored 1.0 across 12 smoke steps score 0.0 at 500 graded steps, at a mean delta of minus 2.265135 nats and a t statistic of minus 27.559669?

Method: The per-batch domain composition produced by the shipped policy was enumerated at the graded profile without touching the accelerator, and compared against the global domain proportions and against a uniform shuffle.

Result: The residual seat loop in the apportionment granted the whole rounding residual to a single domain instead of one seat per domain. At the smoke profile the residual is one seat, so the loop coincides with correct largest remainder apportionment and the defect is invisible. At the graded profile the residual is four seats, so domain zero took four seats of every batch until it was exhausted at batch 112, then the next domain absorbed the residual, and so on. The final batches of the run were pure single-domain blocks of 16 sequences. The mean number of distinct domains per batch was 6.372 against 6.766 for a uniform shuffle, and the mean squared composition deviation from the global proportions was worse than the comparator rather than better.

Consequence: The policy was not a stratification at the graded scale, it was a domain-blocked schedule with a single-domain tail, which is the known-wrong route known_wrong_identity_order arriving through a rounding bug. Exact largest remainder apportionment was implemented in solution/reference_order.py, which lifted the mean distinct domains per batch to 7.876 with a minimum of 7, and cut the mean squared composition deviation from 13.7203 to 1.3603 against 13.7203 for a uniform shuffle.

### kernel-nondeterminism-floor

Question: How much of the measured improvement is the ordering, and how much is run to run kernel noise at a fixed seed and a fixed order?

Method: The identical arm, at the identical graded seed and the identical order, was executed twice at the graded profile and the final validation losses were compared.

Result: Under the default kernels the same fixed seed under the same fixed order produced final validation losses that differed by about 0.018 nats between repeats, which is the same size as the entire ordering effect this task grades. The tied embedding backward and the fused attention backward both accumulate through atomics. Under torch.use_deterministic_algorithms with tf32 disabled, two repeats produced bit identical losses at no measurable cost in wall clock.

Consequence: Kernel determinism was made part of the frozen substrate and is enforced in environment/runner/run_s03.py train_one, which runs after the submitted ordering module has been imported, so a submission cannot flip the flags back and reintroduce the lottery. Without this the comparator ensemble measures kernel noise rather than ordering, and no reported score is reproducible on re-measurement.

### eight-shuffle-lottery-discrimination

Question: Does the graded operating point separate a derived ordering from a lucky random shuffle, which the smoke scale demonstrably did not?

Method: The closing experiment recorded under the coverage gap lottery-discrimination-unmeasured was run on the accelerator. The graded profile was run with the submitted arm replaced in turn by eight independent fixed uniform random shuffles, across all three graded seeds, against the same six comparator draws, and the grader statistic was computed for each.

Result: Across the eight draws the mean delta averaged minus 0.000603 nats with a between-permutation standard deviation of 0.003328 nats, and the largest single draw reached only plus 0.003380 nats. Seven of the eight failed the gate. One draw passed at a mean delta of plus 0.003380 nats and a t statistic of 2.966 because its six pair spread happened to be unusually tight, which is a false positive rate of one in eight at the shipped threshold. The reference ordering sits at plus 0.018097 nats, which is 5.6 between-permutation standard deviations above the centre of that distribution and 4.4 times the best of the eight draws.

Consequence: The gap lottery-discrimination-unmeasured is closed as MEASURED. The graded operating point separates the reference from the shuffle ensemble by a wide margin, which the 12 step smoke scale did not, where a lucky draw scored a full 1.0 at a t statistic of 7.99. The residual one in eight false positive at the authored threshold of 2.0 is recorded honestly as the new and narrower coverage gap comparator-false-positive-rate-one-in-eight rather than papered over by raising the threshold after seeing the draws.

### frequency-tilt-direction

Question: Beyond exact apportionment, is there a further ordering effect at fixed multiset, and does it have a direction or is it seed noise?

Method: Five orderings were run at the graded profile across the three graded seeds against the same comparator ensemble: exact apportionment alone, apportionment with the within-domain queue in ascending entropy, in descending entropy, and apportionment with a quadratic frequency tilt on the per-domain release curve run at both signs. The tilt leaves the aggregate release exactly linear, so batch sizes and the consumed multiset are untouched.

Result: Exact apportionment alone measured plus 0.008418 nats at a t statistic of 1.386, which does not clear the gate. Ascending entropy measured plus 0.001439 and descending entropy plus 0.009455. The tilt that releases the rarer domains earlier measured plus 0.018097 nats at a t statistic of 10.339167, and the opposite tilt, releasing the rarer domains later, measured minus 0.001934 nats and was worse than the comparator ensemble. The two signs of the same construction landing on opposite sides of the comparator is what establishes the effect has a direction rather than being seed noise.

Consequence: The tilt at the sign that helped is the third component of the reference policy. It was then checked on three seeds that played no part in selecting it, seeds 3, 4 and 5, which are not graded seeds, and measured plus 0.034875, plus 0.017510 and plus 0.023403 nats against their own comparator draws, so the effect is not an artefact of the three graded seeds. The tilt magnitude is not tuned against the reward: it is fixed at the largest value for which every per-domain release curve stays monotone.

## Rejected routes and their known-wrong controls

- `known_wrong_entropy_curriculum`: Sort the corpus by unigram_entropy and train easy to hard, which is the widely claimed curriculum result. The comparator is a uniform shuffle, not the domain-blocked corpus order, so an entropy sort changes which sequences are adjacent without removing the batch composition variance the frozen optimizer must absorb. Design intent, UNMEASURED.
- `known_wrong_identity_order`: Return the corpus in natural index order, which is domain-blocked because the generator emits domain by domain. Domain-blocked batches maximize the composition variance the comparator already averages away, so this is expected to be worse than the comparator rather than better. Design intent, UNMEASURED.
- `known_wrong_reshuffle`: Return a different random shuffle. The comparator is an ensemble of uniform shuffles, so a second shuffle has an expected improvement of exactly zero against it. MEASURED AT THE GRADED SCALE over eight independent draws: the mean delta across the eight averaged minus 0.000603 nats with a between-permutation standard deviation of 0.003328, the best single draw reached plus 0.003380, and seven of the eight failed the gate. The experiment is recorded in full under measured_experiments as eight-shuffle-lottery-discrimination, and the one draw in eight that passed is carried honestly as the coverage gap comparator-false-positive-rate-one-in-eight.
- `known_wrong_reverse_frequency_tilt`: Apply the frequency tilt in the direction that releases the rarer domains later, on the intuition that the underfit domains should be seen last so they are freshest at evaluation. MEASURED AT THE GRADED SCALE: this sign of the tilt measured a mean delta of minus 0.001934 nats and was worse than the comparator ensemble, while the opposite sign measured plus 0.018097. The intuition is the recall-shaped one and it is the wrong way round at this operating point, so the direction has to be measured rather than assumed.
- `known_wrong_non_permutation`: Repeat a small high-value subset and drop the rest. The INVARIANT checker reads the consumed multiset digest accumulated across the whole run and refuses anything that is not the frozen index set exactly once.

## Defeat mechanism

Curriculum and data-ordering gains are widely claimed and rarely reproduce. The comparator here is an ensemble of uniform random shuffles rather than the domain-blocked corpus order, so the recalled move of shuffling has an expected improvement of exactly zero and was measured at plus 0.003380 nats in its luckiest of eight draws, and the second recalled move of sorting easy to hard measured plus 0.001439 nats and does not clear the gate. What is left is a construction at fixed multiset whose components must be derived and whose direction must be measured rather than recalled: the reference's own frequency tilt is worse than the comparator at the sign that intuition suggests and better at the other, and the exact apportionment that the whole design rests on differs from a plausible near-miss implementation only in a rounding residual that is invisible at any small operating point. It must then clear a threshold on a statistic whose noise scale is estimated in band from graded seeds and comparator draws the ordering policy never sees.
