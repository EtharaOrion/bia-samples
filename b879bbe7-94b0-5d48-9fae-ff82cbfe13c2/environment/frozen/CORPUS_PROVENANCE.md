## What the corpus is

Open the shard named below, skip its 1024-byte header, read little-endian uint16 GPT-2 BPE token ids from the carrier's own token offset, split on the end-of-text token 50256 to recover upstream document boundaries, decode each document with the GPT-2 BPE vocabulary, and join the documents with a blank line under the preamble line recorded below. The document count starts at 256 and doubles until the joined text reaches size_floor_bytes, which is what fixes the length. Every carrier is cut from its own disjoint region of the shard, spaced by carrier_stride_tokens, so no two carriers in this slot share a byte. Nothing is sampled, shuffled or synthesized.

The collection is FineWeb10B, external source https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb, staged by https://github.com/KellerJordan/modded-nanogpt data/cached_fineweb10B.py. The declaration of record is `environment/frozen/data_config.json` and the substrate declaration is `environment/nanogpt_substrate.json`. The bytes were emitted by seed/forge/substrate.
