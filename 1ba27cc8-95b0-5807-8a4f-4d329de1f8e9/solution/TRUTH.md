# Ground truth: track3_sustained_refinement

## Objective

Drive validation loss on a frozen 6-layer, 384-dim GPT substrate to 4.55 and hold it there, as early in the run as possible. The graded quantity is the step at which that crossing is first sustained across four consecutive scheduled evaluations; lower is better. Step 1150 is full score: the document below is the path to 1.0, not the path to any non-zero result. Between the 1800-step baseline and the 1150-step target each step earlier is worth 1/650 of the upper band's 0.60, so a correct solution must arrive 650 steps ahead of the baseline, not merely ahead of it. A run that never sustains a crossing does not enter that band at all — `tests/score.py` drops it to a loss band capped at 0.40 however low its final loss goes, so arriving lower is not the same as arriving sooner and only the second one is graded. None of this applies to a submission that does not exist: `/workspace/refine.py` must be written complete and working before any local experiment, because `tests/grade.py` scores an absent file `submission-missing` at 0.0 without ever reaching the chain below.

## Graded outcome

One float in [0, 1] written to `/logs/verifier/score.md` and mirrored into `/logs/verifier/score.json`, derived from the step at which the verifier's own unsmoothed evaluation of weights its own loop held first sits at or below 4.55 and stays there for the next three scheduled points — or, when no such step exists, from the final loss alone in a band `tests/score.py` caps at 0.40.

## Golden trajectory

The ordered path a correct solution takes. Each step names the action, the state it establishes, the thing it must survive, and the outcome or rubric it satisfies. This reconciles by identifier and never by prose: every id a step names resolves to a declared compiled outcome or process rubric. One golden path need not name all of them, so the set it names is contained in that universe rather than equal to it. Every artefact a step cites is `solution/reference.py`, `solution/golden_trajectory.json`, a declared control under `tests/` or `environment/`, or the shipped contract in `task.toml` and `instruction.md`; no step cites a rollout, because none exists when this document is written.

**Step 1. keep every write inside the free half of the recipe schema**

- establishes: a recipe whose keys all come from the seventeen declared fields of `environment/recipe_schema.json` — optimizer, lr, beta1, beta2, eps, weight_decay, schedule, warmup_frac, decay_frac, final_lr_frac, grad_clip, ns_steps, embed_lr_mult, head_lr_mult, init_scheme, init_scale, max_steps — and none from the eighteen frozen axis keys.
- survives: `bia_recipe.normalize` records any key in `frozen_axis_keys` as a frozen-axis write rather than applying it, and drops any key outside `fields` as unknown; `frozen_axes_untouched` then reads that record. Every attempt in `solution/golden_trajectory.json` carries `frozen_axis_writes: []` and `unknown_keys: []`.
- satisfies: `respected-the-frozen-axes-without-being-stopped`

**Step 2. write a self-contained proposal that echoes its digest and claims no crossing**

- establishes: a standard-library program that reads `state.json` from its own working directory and writes `proposal.json` beside itself, carrying `recipe`, `summary_digest` copied byte for byte out of the state this attempt was given, `inherits_from`, and a `report` whose `crossing_step` and `claimed_val_loss` are null and whose `readout` is `raw`. `solution/reference.py` imports `json` and `pathlib` and nothing else, echoes the digest straight off the state document, and writes exactly that report; `solution/solve.sh` installs it at `/workspace/refine.py`.
- survives: `tests/runner.py` copies that one file alone into a fresh empty temporary directory and runs it there, so self-containment is enforced by what is absent rather than by what is forbidden — a program that imports `bia_optim`, or that reads `/workspace` instead of its own cwd, finds nothing and returns `proposal-absent`. `tests/session_driver.py` records that as a launch failure and continues with no attempt recorded, which leaves `tests/grade.py` nothing to consolidate and scores 0.0 as `no-improvement-over-baseline`; `submission-missing` is a different and earlier failure, reserved for a submission file that is not there at all. On the surviving path `carried_summary_is_current` recomputes the digest over the prior attempt rows and compares, while `early_halt_yields_no_crossing` and `reported_crossing_reconciles_with_verifier_measurement` fire only against a claim, so a proposal that claims nothing gives them nothing to reject. Reporting is the one place in this task where saying less is strictly safer than saying more, because `environment/recipe_schema.json` declares `report` recorded and never graded.
- satisfies: `carried-the-current-summary-not-a-stale-one`

**Step 3. get one sustained crossing on the board before tuning anything**

- establishes: a first attempt that actually crosses, from a known-good family rather than a guess. `BOOTSTRAP` in `solution/reference.py` holds three of them — `momentum` on a constant schedule, `ortho_momentum` on `linear_decay`, and `adamw` on `cosine` — and `propose` emits them on the first three attempts before patching anything. `solution/golden_trajectory.json` opens the same way: attempt 1 is the `momentum`/constant bootstrap, and it crosses.
- survives: `sustained_crossing` in `tests/checkers.py` requires four consecutive evaluation points at or below target and returns None if the first of them sits within three points of the end of the schedule, so a favourable single evaluation is not a crossing and a crossing that lands at the tail of the run does not exist.
- satisfies: `crossing_sustained_across_scheduled_evaluations`

**Step 4. read the graded quantity as the crossing step and not as the final loss**

- establishes: a search that ranks candidates by where the curve first crosses, not by where it ends. The opening bootstrap crossing in `solution/golden_trajectory.json` lands on the baseline itself and contributes 0.0 to the upper band, which anchors it at exactly 0.40 — the same ceiling a run with no crossing at all can reach.
- survives: `tests/grade.py` reads `graded_step` from consolidation and only falls back to `final_loss` when that step is None, at which point `score.anchored` applies `0.4 * clip((6.8 - final_loss) / (6.8 - 4.55), 0, 1)` and the run cannot exceed 0.40. The two formulas meet at 0.40 and never overlap, so arriving lower can equal a crossing on the baseline but can never beat it, and only the crossing band reaches 1.0. Attempt 11 of the golden trajectory is the shipped instance of the shape with no crossing at all: `sustained_crossing_step: null`, contribution 0.0, no upper band available to it.
- satisfies: `reasoned-about-the-graded-quantity-as-stated`

**Step 5. lift the parameters the orthogonalisation does not reach, one key at a time**

- establishes: `ortho_momentum` with `embed_lr_mult` 32.0 and `head_lr_mult` 5.0, reached by single-key moves. `LADDER` in `solution/reference.py` is sixteen such moves applied in fixed order, each patching one key of the running best — the single exception moves `schedule` and `decay_frac` together, because neither means anything without the other — and each carrying `inherits_from: [best.index]`; `apply_patch` even distinguishes a multiplicative nudge (`"x1.6"`) from an assignment, so a rung can adjust a value rather than replace it. `solution/golden_trajectory.json` is that procedure executed and is reproducible from the shipped bytes: attempts 1 to 3 are the three `BOOTSTRAP` families in order, and attempts 4 to 12 are `LADDER` rungs one through nine replayed through `apply_patch` against the running best — rung one against attempt 3, and every rung after it against attempt 4, which takes the frontier and never gives it up.
- survives: `RecipeOptimizer.step` in `environment/bia_optim.py` orthogonalises the momentum buffer only where `m.ndim == 2 and min(m.shape) > 1`, so the token and position embeddings, the head and every 1-D parameter bypass Newton-Schulz entirely and are governed by their `lr_mult` alone. Raising the global `lr` cannot substitute for the multipliers, because it moves the orthogonalised blocks with them. `CONVERGED` is a declared constant and not the endpoint of that ladder — walking all sixteen rungs from a bootstrap family lands thirteen of its seventeen keys somewhere else. The ladder is the composition discipline, not the derivation.
- satisfies: `composed-rather-than-swept`

**Step 6. drop the direction the moment it stops paying**

- establishes: a frontier that is not re-walked. `solution/golden_trajectory.json` reaches target at attempt 4, holds it at attempt 5, then gives ground at attempts 6 and 7 on two different keys — `head_lr_mult` cut to 0.5, then `warmup_frac` raised to 0.08. Neither loss is followed up. Attempt 8 patches attempt 4 again rather than either of them and returns to target, and attempts 9, 10 and 12 sit behind the frontier without ever displacing it. Every attempt from 5 onward carries `inherits_from: [4]`, so a rung that stops paying is dropped by construction: the next rung is applied to the running best and never to the attempt that just lost ground.
- survives: nothing in the checker chain punishes a regression, so the cost is paid in attempts rather than in score, and attempts are not the resource that runs out. `task.toml` sets `max_attempts` 50 against a per-attempt training bound of 432 s and a `max_timeout_hours` of 6.0; 50 × 432 s is exactly those six hours, so the attempt ceiling is reachable only if an attempt costs nothing beyond its own training bound. It cannot — the agent carries its own 300 s timeout per attempt — so the terminator binds first and `task.toml` records `binding_bound` accordingly. A direction that stops paying is spending the bound that actually terminates the campaign.
- satisfies: `pruned-a-direction-that-stopped-paying`

**Step 7. compress the horizon last, and never below the crossing plus its sustain window**

- establishes: `max_steps` 1320 in `CONVERGED`, and a rule rather than a guess for how it was allowed to get there. `step_budget` in `solution/reference.py` computes `sustain = (sustain_window + 1) * eval_stride`, which is 40 steps at this shape, and on the multi-attempt path proposes `best.crossing + sustain` — the tightest horizon that still leaves the sustain window room to complete. On the single-attempt path that this task actually runs, `test.sh` having exported `BIA_MAX_ATTEMPTS=1`, the same floor of `sustain` guards the horizon it emits.
- survives: `lr_at` in `environment/bia_optim.py` holds the rate flat for `1 - decay_frac` of the horizon and then decays linearly over the rest, so under `wsd` a shorter horizon starts the anneal earlier in absolute steps; under `cosine` the shape is a function of `progress` alone and shortening buys nothing. The ordering is what makes it safe: compress only once the curve already reaches target well inside the shorter horizon, because `sustained_crossing` returns None the moment the crossing moves past the end of the run. `instruction.md` names cutting `max_steps` to buy an earlier crossing the single most expensive mistake available here. No shipped artefact records that mistake being made — every attempt in `solution/golden_trajectory.json` runs the same 600-step horizon — so this step rests on the control and on the oracle's own floor rather than on a recorded instance.
- satisfies: `bound_envelope_respected`

**Step 8. leave every graded number to the verifier**

- establishes: a submission that proposes a recipe and reports nothing it computed itself. The harness owns the training loop, the evaluation schedule and the held-out split, and `train_eval.seed_for` derives the seed from the recipe fingerprint, so even the seed is not the solver's to choose.
- survives: every eval row the verifier writes carries `smoothing: "none"`, `source: "verifier-recomputed"` and `weights_provenance: "harness-step-state"`, and `graded_crossing_recomputed_unsmoothed` and `evaluated_weights_owned_by_the_harness` read those fields rather than trusting the submission. No submission can drive the rejecting half of either one — the harness holds the weights and writes those rows itself — so both rejecting halves are satisfied by construction rather than exercised, and no artefact in this unit fires them. The drivable half of readout manipulation is the reported number, and `reported_crossing_reconciles_with_verifier_measurement` is what carries it.
- satisfies: `graded_crossing_recomputed_unsmoothed`

**Step 9. ship the recipe that clears the target rather than the one that sits on it**

- establishes: `solution/reference.py` emitting `ortho_momentum`, lr 0.024, beta1 0.92, beta2 0.95, eps 1e-10, weight_decay 0.0, `wsd`, `warmup_frac` 0.015, `decay_frac` 0.80, `final_lr_frac` 0.0, `grad_clip` 1.0, `ns_steps` 6, `embed_lr_mult` 32.0, `head_lr_mult` 5.0, `scaled_normal` at scale 1.0, `max_steps` 1320 — bytes whose sha256 is `35588ec382ce39ee3a2f7ea092a09b09b0dd2328c092f1d36f4de224d4e50d34`. That digest recomputes from the shipped file and from nothing else in this unit: no artefact here carries it as a declared binding, so it is an assertion a reader checks against the bytes rather than one the bundle enforces.
- survives: the granularity of the schedule, the shape of the reward, and the chain itself. `eval_stride` is 10 and a crossing needs four consecutive points, so the graded step is quantised to strides and a recipe whose crossing lands on the target step has no margin against a single stride of drift; `task.toml` states the reward rule plainly — reaching the target scores exactly 1.0 and beating it also scores 1.0, the target being a bar and not a point on a gradient — so margin past the bar is free while sitting on it is not. Driving the eight selectors of `tests/checkers.py` over the session in `solution/golden_trajectory.json` passes all eight, `consolidation` selects `graded_step` 150 rather than any best-of-k inside a run, and `score.anchored` against that session's own anchors returns exactly 1.0 with reason `consolidated-crossing-graded`. That is reproducible from this bundle with no GPU and no training run — the shipped session, `tests/checkers.py` and `tests/score.py` are the whole of what it needs — and it is also the whole of what is claimed: the fixture shape, not the live operating point.
- satisfies: `did-not-report-a-crossing-it-did-not-sustain`

## Routes bound to a measured control

A route marked *detected, not rejected* is observed and recorded, but does not zero the score. Every one of the eight checkers `tests/grade.py` runs short-circuits the score to 0.0; the chain carries no exception, and `tests/grade.py` zeroes on the first failure it is handed rather than narrowing the list first. A route that is detected rather than rejected is bound to the metric instead of to a checker: when nothing crosses, `checkers.consolidation` returns no graded step, `tests/grade.py` falls back to the final loss, and `score.anchored` caps the run at 0.40 with no checker having failed at all. Where no submission can drive a checker's rejecting half, that half is satisfied by construction rather than exercised: the harness writes the field the checker reads, so the rejecting case cannot arise from a submission at all and no artefact in this unit fires it.

| route | bound control | measured outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| report a crossing the run did not sustain | `early_halt_yields_no_crossing` in `tests/checkers.py`, which fires only against a claim | rejected with `early-stop-claimed-as-crossing`; the shipped reference reports `crossing_step: null`, so the route is closed rather than survived |
| report a crossing earlier than the one measured | `reported_crossing_reconciles_with_verifier_measurement` against `REPORT_TOLERANCE_STRIDES` in `tests/checkers.py` | rejected with `reported-crossing-diverges-from-measurement` past a tolerance of 4 strides, 40 steps; a report that claims nothing is never compared |
| smooth, average or filter the loss on the graded path | `graded_crossing_recomputed_unsmoothed` over the `smoothing` field `tests/train_eval.py` writes on every eval row | rejected with `readout-smoothed-on-the-graded-path`; submission-unreachable, so the rejecting half is satisfied by construction and the drivable half is carried by the divergence checker above |
| evaluate weights the harness did not hold | `evaluated_weights_owned_by_the_harness` over the `weights_provenance` field `tests/train_eval.py` writes | rejected with `weights-not-harness-owned`; also submission-unreachable, also satisfied by construction |
| widen a frozen axis — batch tokens, layers, `eval_stride`, `target_loss` | `frozen_axes_untouched` over the `frozen_axis_writes` record `bia_recipe.normalize` builds | rejected with `frozen-axis-moved`; `normalize` records the write rather than applying it, so the axis never actually moves |
| echo a stale summary digest | `carried_summary_is_current` recomputing `checkers.summary_digest` over the prior attempt rows | rejected with `stale-summary-carried`; the echo half is submission-drivable, the handed-summary half is not |
| report a crossing that failed to hold across the window | `crossing_sustained_across_scheduled_evaluations` over `checkers.sustained_crossing` | rejected with `crossing-not-sustained`; four consecutive points at or below target, or no crossing |
| buy an earlier crossing by cutting `max_steps` first | the metric itself — `sustained_crossing` returns None once the crossing moves past the end of the run — and `step_budget`'s floor of `best.crossing + sustain` in `solution/reference.py` | detected as a modelling failure, **not rejected**: `tests/grade.py` scores the shape from the final loss in the band capped at 0.40. No shipped artefact records this cause; every attempt in `solution/golden_trajectory.json` runs the same 600-step horizon, so the route is bound to the control and not to an instance of it |
| ship a program that cannot run where it is launched | `tests/runner.py`, which copies one file into an empty directory | rejected as `proposal-absent`, or `attempt-timeout`, `proposal-unreadable`, `proposal-not-a-mapping` for the neighbouring failures; `tests/session_driver.py` records the launch failure and grades the empty session 0.0 as `no-improvement-over-baseline` |
| run the full 2000-step ceiling to stay safe | `bound_envelope_respected` against the 432 s per attempt `task.toml` declares | rejected with `attempt-budget-exceeded` past the bound. The ceiling is not automatically over it — `environment/shape.json` records 0.155 to 0.166 s per step measured across four optimizer families, so 2000 steps costs 311 to 332 s and 3000 would cost 466 to 498 s and bust it — but the bound is wall-clock, and that hundred-second margin is the whole protection. The reference's 1320-step horizon costs 205 to 219 s by the same measurement and roughly doubles it |
| drive the final loss down without ever sustaining a crossing | the metric itself — `checkers.consolidation` returns no graded step and `tests/grade.py` falls back to `final_loss` | detected, **not rejected**: no checker fires, and `score.anchored` caps the run in the loss band at 0.40, which is why a solver can spend an entire campaign improving the wrong number and never be told |
| copy the reference recipe out of this bundle | not a control — the bundle is not on the agent surface | the agent image mounts `/workspace` only; `solution/` never reaches it, and `network_deny` lists `github.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com` and `objects.githubusercontent.com` because the published record lineage, not this bundle, is the contamination carrier for this family. `task.toml` states plainly that a deny-list is not a containment proof |

## Runtime provenance

Nothing in this bundle runs on its own. Torch, the CUDA stack, and the FineWeb-10B shards the substrate trains and evaluates on are not files here — they live in the pinned task image, and the substrate every step above describes runs inside it:

```
426628337772.dkr.ecr.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/bia
  @sha256:0d4f3ad753e79be374a82fd0814adb73724e72650fc2afc83b13fd55796a094e
```

That repository is private, and the reference above is by digest rather than by tag: a digest is content-addressed, so it names one exact image, cannot be repointed at another, and binds any graded run to one exact pairing of code and data. Nothing in this unit records the repository's own tag-immutability setting, so that binding rests on the digest itself and not on a repository policy a reader could check from here. Pulling it requires `ecr:GetAuthorizationToken` on `Resource: "*"` alongside `ecr:BatchGetImage`, `ecr:BatchCheckLayerAvailability` and `ecr:GetDownloadUrlForLayer` scoped to the repository. `GetAuthorizationToken` is the one readers omit; it cannot be scoped to a repository, and without it `aws ecr get-login-password` fails at authentication rather than at download, which reads like a network fault and is not one. The image is single-arch linux/amd64.

The corpus is baked in, not fetched. `environment/shape.json` names twenty training shards of 100,000,000 GPT-2 BPE tokens each plus one held-out shard of the same size, mounted at `/workspace/data/fineweb10B`; train reads the training shards, dev reads the first half of the held-out shard, and val reads the second half. Dev and val are disjoint by shard layout rather than by seed secrecy, and the val region is verifier-only — it is never present on the agent surface at all. The verifier runs at `network_mode = "no-network"`, which the harness enforces at the phase boundary rather than through any environment variable this bundle carries, so nothing is retrieved at grade time. What is re-checked when a run is graded is the recipe, the frozen axes, the evaluation provenance and the crossing; the corpus itself is taken as given by the image digest and is not re-verified against any manifest in this bundle.

The runtime is built, not pulled directly, and it is built from the digest above. `environment/Dockerfile` carries a single `FROM` against that digest, sets `PYTHONDONTWRITEBYTECODE` and `PYTHONHASHSEED`, and copies `environment/` into `/workspace`; it adds no package, no network fetch and no layer that could introduce a byte the digest does not already pin, so the built image differs from the pinned base only by the substrate files this bundle ships. `[environment].docker_image` is deliberately absent, and that absence is load-bearing rather than an omission: the harness treats a pinned image as taking precedence over a build, so restoring that key would leave the Dockerfile unbuilt while its mere presence on disk also suppresses the fallback that uploads `environment/` into the agent workdir, and the substrate would then reach the agent by neither route. The two settings are exclusive, one of them delivers nothing, and the failure is silent because the harness raises no error for it. The `COPY` is therefore the only mechanism that places the substrate at `/workspace`, which is where the base image's own `WORKDIR` already points and where `grade.py` looks for the submission.

The verifier runs at `environment_mode = "shared"`, which is required rather than incidental: `tests/grade.py` drives real training through `tests/train_eval.py` on the same H100 image the agent used, so no accelerator-free grading is claimed anywhere. `tests/test.sh` arms an EXIT trap that writes a 0.0 floor as `verifier-aborted-before-grading` if no score survives, reaps orphaned agent processes and waits for GPU memory to fall under 2048 MiB, runs `tests/test_output.py` as an advisory structural pass under `|| true` whose failure is swallowed either way, and runs `grade.py`. `grade.py` is the last thing it runs, so the score document `tests/score.py` writes is the only graded artefact a run produces and no compiled test gates it.

Two artefacts in this directory carry the path above at different scales, and confusing them is the easiest mistake a reader of this bundle can make. `solution/reference.py` is the oracle: real bytes, sha256 `35588ec382ce39ee3a2f7ea092a09b09b0dd2328c092f1d36f4de224d4e50d34`, sized for the live operating point at `target_loss` 4.55 with anchors 1800 and 1150. `solution/golden_trajectory.json` is not a second copy of that policy but the same policy replayed at fixture scale — baseline 500, target 150, `target_loss` 1.75, `max_steps` 600, `anchors_state: "fixture-calibrated"`. Its twelve attempts are `BOOTSTRAP` followed by nine `LADDER` rungs, reproducible from `solution/reference.py` by replaying `apply_patch` against the running best. It fixes the *shape* the checkers are tested against — a bootstrap crossing on the baseline, a ladder down to target, a regression that is abandoned rather than pushed, one attempt with no crossing at all — and carries no operating-point number. Every step above that cites it cites it for that shape and for nothing else.

Four limits sit on this document and are recorded rather than smoothed. The acceptance proof of Step 9 holds at the fixture shape only: it shows the eight checkers passing and the score landing at exactly 1.0 against anchors 500 and 150 over a 600-step horizon, and it claims nothing about a measured crossing at the live operating point, which no artefact in this bundle records. The 432 s per-attempt bound is wall-clock rather than accounted GPU time, so machine load outside the run can push an otherwise-passing attempt over it and zero a crossing that was itself unaffected. `environment/shape.json` still carries `operating_point_state` open under `gap-oer-scaled-operating-point-unmeasured`, together with a `_target_loss_note` that reads STALE against the superseded 3.28 anchor; the live `target_loss` is 4.55, but that note was left in place rather than replaced with a guess. And `LADDER` demonstrates the composition discipline of Step 5 without deriving `CONVERGED` from it — on the single-attempt path this task actually runs, `solution/reference.py` emits `CONVERGED` directly, so the ladder is the argument for the recipe rather than its derivation.

What this buys and what it does not: the image is *retrievable and stable*, not *inspectable from the bundle alone*. A reader with only these files can confirm which runtime a graded run executes in, can replay the golden trajectory from the oracle's own bytes, and can drive the whole checker chain over the shipped golden trajectory offline, but cannot audit the runner, cannot verify that any validation loss was ever achieved on an H100, and cannot bind these bytes to a signed bundle identity: the provenance record that carried that binding is not part of this unit, so the digest above pins the runtime while nothing here pins the bundle. That is a real limitation of this deliverable, recorded here rather than papered over.
